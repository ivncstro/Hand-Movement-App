##  Ideas / Thoughts
> Random ideas, future features, improvements

- MediaPipe vs. OpenCV
OpenCV is what I'm currently using to open my webcam, capture frames, and display video.
MediaPipe can analyze those frames and find 21 points (landmarks) on a hand.
I can use both together: OpenCV handles the camera, while MediaPipe detects the hand.
- Video vs Live_Stream
VIDEO processes frames one at a time and gives the results directly.
LIVE_STREAM processes frames asynchronously and uses a callback function to return the results.
